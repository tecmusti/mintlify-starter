estudjo is an AI platform that turns your product photos into polished marketing images, short videos, and ready-to-publish product descriptions. Use the REST API to bring AI generation into your e-commerce pipeline, automation tools, or applications — send your product image URLs, and get studio-quality output back.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your API key">
    Sign in to the estudjo dashboard at [estudjo.com](https://www.estudjo.com) and generate an API key from your account settings. See [Authentication](/authentication).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Queue a generation">
    Send a `POST /generate` request with your product image URLs and a model — plus a scene or your own prompt for images and videos. You'll receive a `task_id` in the response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your results">
    Poll `GET /status?taskId={task_id}` until `status` is `completed`, or pass a `callBackUrl` to have estudjo POST the result to you. Your files are in the `result_urls` array.
  </Step>
</Steps>
